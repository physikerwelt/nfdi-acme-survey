Status: alpha 01. First version; expect problems and review the host lists before relying on the results.

Which certificate authorities issue the TLS certificates of services run by the consortia of the German National Research Data Infrastructure (NFDI)? The 26 consortia (Wikidata instances of Q98270496, accepted NFDI consortium), Base4NFDI (Q113544452) and NFDI e.V. with its directorate (Q61658497) and their official websites (P856) are taken from Wikidata and cross-checked with the DFG project database GEPRIS (P4870). Hostnames under each organisation's own domains are taken from certificate transparency logs via crt.sh (unexpired certificates); service hosts under institutional domains were added from Wikidata and the organisations' websites and reviewed by hand (services.json in the code). For each hostname, the certificate currently served on port 443 is recorded.

Files: organisations.csv (organisations with Wikidata QIDs, role, websites and surveyed domains), hosts.csv (one row per hostname with organisation and certificate issuer), summary.csv (per organisation), issuers.csv (per certificate authority), run.json (provenance, totals and the code commit). The code of the survey is cited by its Software Heritage ID.

Limitations: service discovery covers only what Wikidata and the organisations' websites list; hosts without publicly logged certificates are not covered.
