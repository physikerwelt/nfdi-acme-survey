Status: alpha 01. First version; expect problems and review the host lists before relying on the results.

Which certificate authorities issue the TLS certificates of services run by the consortia of the German National Research Data Infrastructure (NFDI)? The 26 consortia (Wikidata instances of Q98270496, accepted NFDI consortium), Base4NFDI (Q113544452) and NFDI e.V. with its directorate (Q61658497) and their official websites (P856) are taken from Wikidata and cross-checked with the DFG project database GEPRIS (P4870). Hostnames under each consortium domain are taken from certificate transparency logs via crt.sh (unexpired certificates). For each hostname, the certificate currently served on port 443 is recorded.

Files: organisations.csv (organisations with Wikidata QIDs, role, websites and surveyed domains), hosts.csv (one row per hostname with organisation and certificate issuer), summary.csv (per organisation), issuers.csv (per certificate authority), run.json (provenance and totals), and the source code of the survey script.

Limitations: services under institutional domains (e.g. university or library hosts) are only included where they are an organisation's official website; hosts without publicly logged certificates are not covered.
